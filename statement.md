# Project Statement

## Problem Statement

Students often lose personal belongings around a campus and may not know where to report them. Found items can also remain unclaimed because there is no single place to record and search them. The Campus Lost & Found System provides one simple place to report lost and found items, search records, and handle claims.

## Scope

The project covers student accounts, item reports, searching, claim requests, claim review, and basic statistics. It is designed for a college campus and stores data locally in SQLite.

## Target Users

- Students
- Campus administrators or staff

## High-Level Features

1. User registration and login
2. Lost/found item reporting
3. Item search and filtering
4. Claim submission
5. Claim tracking
6. Admin approval/rejection
7. Basic statistics
